Early praise

The question

Which Jesus is grounded in the earliest evidence?

Two billion people have been taught a Jesus who was never crucified, never called the Son of God, and never rose from the dead. Most of the Christians around them have never been shown where that portrait came from.

The Borrowed Christ is a historical investigation, not a polemic. It works from dates, manuscripts and the ordinary tools of source criticism — and it grades its own evidence, lowering its confidence where the manuscripts require it.

"The Jesus of the Qur'ān is borrowed and His Christology is cut out, drawn not from the first-century witnesses to Jesus of Nazareth, but from the Syriac-Aramaic apocryphal traditions of the second through seventh centuries."

Introduction: The Investigation

Begin here, because everything that follows runs through this place.

Three miles south of Jerusalem, on the road to Bethlehem, there stands an octagonal church built around a rock. Pilgrims call it the Kathisma — the Seat — because tradition says Mary rested on that stone when the journey became too much for her. On her feast day the church fills. Lamps are lit. A deacon reads, and then a cantor sings the story the pilgrims came to hear: how the mother of God, exhausted and in pain, was refreshed when a palm tree bent its branches down to her, and how water broke from the ground at its roots.

Nobody in that building thinks they are hearing Scripture. The story is not in Matthew. It is not in Luke. It is a beloved embellishment, four centuries young, and the bishops who permit it know precisely what it is.

The pilgrims go home. Some of them go a long way home — down the incense roads, through Ghassānid country, into the tent camps and market towns of Arabia, where the story is told again in Syriac, and then in Arabic, to people who have never seen a Gospel in their own language and never will.

Two hundred years later a voice recites, in an Arabic that no one has yet written a grammar for: And the pangs of childbirth drove her to the trunk of a palm tree ... your Lord has placed a rivulet beneath you. And shake toward yourself the trunk of the palm tree; it will drop upon you ripe dates.

And it is no longer an embellishment. It is the word of God, and to doubt it is unbelief.

Joshua B. Lingel

Joshua Lingel is the founder and president of i2 Ministries and dean of Mission Muslim World University, which trains more than 1,700 students across seventy nations.
