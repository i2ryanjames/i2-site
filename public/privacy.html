Your privacy

Privacy notice

This notice describes how the i2 Ministries website handles information you choose to send to us.

Last updated September 18, 2026

Messages you send

When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, selected topic, and message so our team can reply. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect the form and a mail delivery provider to deliver the message to our team. Basic request data may be used briefly to limit repeated submissions and protect the form from abuse. Please do not include sensitive personal information in the message.

We use contact messages to respond to your inquiry and manage related correspondence. We do not sell contact form information. Access is limited to people who handle i2 Ministries inquiries and the providers needed to deliver and protect the form.

Donations and other sites

Donation buttons take you to GivingFuel or PayPal. Payment details are entered on those providers' sites, not on this website. Training, ebook, social, and video links may also take you to separate services with their own privacy notices.

Browser storage and videos

We remember your optional video choice in first-party browser storage and load YouTube or Vimeo players only after you allow them or choose to play one video. Details and controls are in our cookie policy.

Questions or requests

To ask about a message you sent, request correction or deletion, or ask another privacy question, email info@i2ministries.org. You can also write to i2 Ministries Inc., PO Box 3403, La Habra, CA 90632 USA.
