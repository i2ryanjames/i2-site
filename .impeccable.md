# Design Context — i2 Ministries (i2-site)

Confirmed with Ryan 2026-09-17.

Updated with Ryan's requests on 2026-09-26: **denominational/network leaders and donors** are the highest-priority audiences. Explain the multiplication model before presenting its training/tools. Ryan explicitly requested an optional homepage routing quiz; this is an exception to the general popup anti-reference below. Use a short, dismissible guide rather than an email gate. Ebook promotions use slim inline banners on informational pages, with no ebook popups anywhere and no ebook banners on Donate or Donate Form. For urgency imagery, use anonymous distant crowds without identifiable faces.

## Audience
Primary: **denominational/network leaders and ministry donors**. Ryan specifically identified African denominational leaders and scholars as a central audience for the homepage guide. Address bishops, pastors, theological educators, and national church leaders as fellow workers who lead the ministry in their own churches and nations. Giving partners also need clear stewardship and a direct way to support the work.
Secondary: local congregations, ministry trainers, missionaries, and students seeking training (Get Trained / MMWU / WISE Global). Use clear English suitable for an international readership; do not assume a US audience.

## Ministry voice
Grounded in the Tanzania Great Commission manuscript, Joshua Lingel's teaching, and the 2024/2026 Forum materials. Use the ministry's own vocabulary: the Great Commission, the Gospel, prayer, evangelism, discipleship, equipping pastors, training those who will train others, and serious study of Christian missions, Islam, and apologetics. Speak with conviction, warmth, and respect for church leadership. Donor language centres on partnership in the Gospel and faithful stewardship. Avoid generic sales-funnel wording such as “find your path,” “readiness,” and “strategic funding conversation.” Keep navigation and privacy instructions plain. The homepage guide already uses this voice in production.

## Jobs to be done
1. Give (card/check via GivingFuel, or PayPal) — the primary conversion.
2. Understand the scale of the need and that i2 has a credible, measurable plan (EMFCI 50/130 countries, funding progress).
3. Verify credibility: Lingel's bio, endorsements, legal/tax facts.
4. Find a training path (secondary).

## Tone: **bold / urgent**
Big numbers, dark high-contrast sections, confident serif display type, strong single CTAs. Urgency without hype —
the numbers carry the weight; no exclamation marks, no countdown gimmicks, no manipulative scarcity.

## Brand decisions (current)
- **Palette: blue** (`styles/theme-shadcn-blue.css`, primary `#3b82f6`, deep `#1e3a8a`, slate darks `#0f172a`). This
  supersedes the warm-gold "Field Warmth" palette described in DESIGN-DECISIONS.md — that doc is historical for color.
- Type: Instrument Serif (display, italic for emphasis words) + DM Sans (body). Do not change fonts.
- Static HTML, no build. Motion = GSAP ScrollTrigger, restrained, must respect reduced-motion.
- Sacred content (never alter): statistics, endorser names/quotes, Lingel bio, Lausanne Covenant, scripture, legal
  text (EIN 76-0800576, PO Box 3403 La Habra), all external + internal URLs. See DESIGN-DECISIONS.md.

## Anti-references (what this site is NOT)
- SaaS landing-page look: gradient blobs, glassmorphism, purple, rounded pill everything, emoji, feature-grid cards.
- Church-bulletin softness: pastel, script fonts, stock photos of praying hands.
- Generic AI layout: everything centered, identical repeating card grids, hero-metric template blocks.
- Anything that reads as a fundraising gimmick (thermometer widgets, fake urgency, pop-ups).

## Floors
Body ≥ 16px, nothing under 14px, 44px tap targets, WCAG AA contrast, no horizontal overflow at 390px, every
image has alt, one visually dominant CTA per screen.
