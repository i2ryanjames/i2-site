Audit priorities

Make the next step easier to find.

  1. Make church leadership and giving visible destinations, and explain how EMFCI, MMWU, WADI and WISE fit together.
  2. Place the plan and a clear response alongside the mission’s urgency message. Its first in-content action currently appears very far down the mobile page.
  3. Remove the repeated payment-method choice and bring the major-gift conversation closer to the funding case.
  4. Fix measured button/link contrast gaps and increase small footer controls to the 44px touch target.

Independent review: 23/40 across ten usability heuristics. The automated scanner’s 196 flags were triaged; many were false positives from old CSS, hidden placeholders and intentional cropping. The new quiz passed all 14 routes, keyboard controls and privacy/persistence checks; 12 repository tests pass.
